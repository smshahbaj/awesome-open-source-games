# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be welcoming, useful, and professional.

Be respectful. Assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and keep criticism constructive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

For sensitive reports, contact the maintainer privately through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile.
