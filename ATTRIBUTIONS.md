- Link to the original project whenever possible.
- Use original, concise descriptions.
- Do not imply ownership, endorsement, or affiliation.

- Do not redistribute third-party assets unless their terms allow it.
- Treat screenshots, logos, artwork, music, trademarks, and other media as separate rights.

The repository's **CC0 1.0** dedication applies to the original material created for this index. It does **not** apply to third-party projects or assets linked from it.

If an entry contains incorrect attribution, ownership, or licensing information, please open an issue or submit a pull request.
